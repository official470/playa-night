Privacy Policy

REAL HOUSE CLUB · web game realhouse.club and the iOS app "REAL HOUSE" · Effective November 17, 2026

Staff AI Co., Ltd. (Realsnap, "we") runs REAL HOUSE CLUB (the web game and the iOS app, the "Service") and processes only the minimum information needed.

1. What we process

  • Everyone: a random visitor ID, the nickname you type, visit and play time, places and stages visited, device type (mobile or not) and dance step count, sent to our analytics database. Game progress (coins, bag, outfit, settings) stays on your device.
  • If you sign in with Apple, Google or Kakao: account identifier, name, email (Google) and profile image URL.
  • Optional profile: photos you upload (2–20) and your Instagram handle (kept private).
  • Optional friends and chat: friend list, chat messages with friends, and your in-game online status and position.

We do not collect precise location, contacts or the advertising identifier, and we do not track you across apps or websites. The motion sensor in the iOS app is used on-device only for shake-to-dance and is never sent to a server.

2. Why

To run the game (sign-in, profiles, friends, chat, showing who is dancing nearby), to understand overall usage (visitor counts, play time, popular stages), and to keep your step record.

3. Who sees what

Other signed-in players can see your nickname, profile photos and in-game status. Your Instagram handle goes only to people you choose. Chats are visible only to the two friends in them.

4. Service providers

Google LLC (Firebase, USA: sign-in, database, analytics), GitHub, Inc. (USA: hosting), Kakao Corp. (Korea: optional sign-in), SoundCloud and YouTube (music playback, under their own policies). Data may be stored in the United States. We never sell personal data or share it for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Account, profile, photos, friends and chats are deleted without delay when you ask to delete your account. Analytics are kept for up to one year. On-device data is removed when you delete the app or clear browser data.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data at any time. Edit or remove your profile and photos in Menu → My profile; delete your account right away in My profile → Delete account.

7. Children

The Service is for ages 17+ (19+ in Korea). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Contact

Staff AI Co., Ltd. operations team — via the in-game 🚩 report button or realhouse.club
